Large language models are trained on broad, general-purpose corpora — but real-world applications demand specialized knowledge. Domain adaptation bridges this gap by steering a pretrained model toward a target domain (e.g., biomedicine, law, finance) without the cost of training from scratch. As models grow larger, doing this efficiently becomes critical.
