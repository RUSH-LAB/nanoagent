# Continual Learning of AI

This chapter provides a comprehensive review of continual learning -- the ability of neural networks to learn from non-stationary data streams while retaining previously acquired knowledge. We cover the problem formulation, taxonomy of approaches, and deep dives into regularization-based, replay-based, architecture-based, and meta-learning methods. We also examine the emerging intersection of continual learning with large language models, discuss standard benchmarks and evaluation protocols, and conclude with open problems and connections to other chapters in this survey.
