# Basics of Machine Learning

This chapter covers the core mathematical machinery of machine learning: the objective functions we optimize, the algorithms that optimize them, and the techniques that ensure models generalize.

- **[Loss Functions](./1-loss-functions.md)**: Cross entropy, KL divergence, and the mathematics of measuring prediction error
- **[RL Algorithms for LLMs](./2-rl-algs.md)**: PPO, DPO, GRPO, and RLOO for reinforcement learning from human feedback
- **[Gradient Descent](./3-gradient-descent.md)**: Optimization algorithms such as SGD, momentum, Adam, and convergence analysis
- **[Backpropagation](./4-backpropagation.md)**: The chain rule on computational graphs, covering the forward pass, backward pass, and gradient flow
- **[Regularization](./5-regularization.md)**: Controlling model complexity through L1/L2 penalties, dropout, early stopping, and the bias-variance tradeoff
