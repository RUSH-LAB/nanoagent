# Calculus and Optimization

Machine learning reduces to finding parameters that minimize a loss function. This chapter covers the mathematics of minimization: multivariable calculus, convex and non-convex optimization, and constrained optimization. The convex case is well understood, but the real difficulty lives in the non-convex and constrained settings, which is why all four sections are needed.

- **[Multivariable Calculus](./1-multivariable-calculus.md)**: Partial derivatives, gradient, directional derivative, Taylor expansion
- **[Convex Optimization](./2-convex-optimization.md)**: Convex sets and functions, convergence guarantees, strong convexity
- **[Non-Convex Optimization](./3-non-convex-optimization.md)**: Saddle points, loss landscape geometry, learning rate schedules
- **[Constrained Optimization](./4-constrained-optimization.md)**: Lagrange multipliers, KKT conditions, duality
