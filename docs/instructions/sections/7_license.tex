\section{License and submission (COMP 480/580)}

Every plugin carries the template's MIT \texttt{LICENSE}, with you as the
copyright holder, and NanoAgent refuses a plugin without it. Publishing on
RUSH Lab needs a public GitHub repository, so a published plugin is open
source under that license.

Giving RUSH Lab permission to open-source your work is optional and does not
affect grading. If you agree, include this statement with your submission:

\begin{quote}
I voluntarily agree that my original contribution to [Project Name] may be
open-sourced under the MIT License. This permission is optional and does not
affect grading.
\end{quote}

To keep your work confidential, do not publish it: email your submission to
[instructor email] and copy [TA emails] instead.
